Olympic Athlete Events SQL Analysis

This analysis is based on historical Olympic data, combining athlete details and their event participation records to extract meaningful insights.
🗂️ Data Sources
athletes table: Contains demographic and personal information of each athlete (e.g., ID, name, team, gender).
athlete_events table: Records details of every Olympic event an athlete has participated in, including:
Sport
Event
Year and season (Summer or Winter)
Medal type (Gold, Silver, Bronze, or NA)

🎯 Objectives of the Analysis
- Analyze individual athlete performance across years and events.
- Determine team and country-level achievements based on medal counts.
- Identify record-breaking or rare accomplishments (e.g., winning all three medals in one Olympics).
- Highlight seasonal versatility (e.g., athletes who won in both Summer and Winter Olympics).
- Detect performance consistency, such as athletes winning gold in consecutive Olympics.
- Track historical milestones, like India’s first medals by type and year.

📌 Key Insights Explored
- Which team has won the most gold medals in total.
- Total silver medals per team and the year of their peak performance.
- Athletes who have only won gold medals (never silver or bronze).
- Top gold medalists per year, including handling of ties.
- India’s first gold, silver, and bronze medals in Olympic history.
- Athletes who won gold in both Summer and Winter Olympics.
- Players who secured all three medals (gold, silver, bronze) in a single Olympic year.
- Identifying athletes with three consecutive Olympic golds in the same event (from 2000 onwards).

📈 Use Cases

- Performance tracking of athletes and teams.
- Sports analytics dashboards for media or reporting.
- Historical exploration of Olympic trends and records.
- Basis for storytelling and data journalism in the context of international sports.
