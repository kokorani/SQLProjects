This SQL file contains a comprehensive set of queries that analyze hotel booking data. It includes table modification, data transformation, and complex analytical queries to extract business insights. The database is assumed to contain the following tables:
hotel_cities
hotel_names
hotel_customers
hotel_bookings

Analytical Queries
1. Top 5 Customers Booking in Their Home City
Finds top 5 customers who made the most bookings in the city where they live. Displays the percentage of those bookings compared to their total bookings.
2. Female Contribution to Revenue and Bookings
Calculates the percentage contribution of female customers in terms of revenue and number of bookings for each hotel.
3. Out-of-State Bookings
Counts the number of bookings where customers traveled from a different state than where the hotel is located.
4. Busiest Day by Hotel
Identifies the date with the highest occupancy for each hotel (excludes checkout date as part of stay).
5. Customers Who Traveled to 3+ States
Finds customers who have booked hotels in at least three different states.
6. Monthly Occupancy Rate per Hotel
Calculates occupancy rate (percentage of rooms booked) for each hotel by month. Assumes 30 days per month.
7. Fully Occupied Dates
Identifies dates when each hotel was fully booked (i.e., number of bookings equals hotel room capacity).
8. Top-Performing Booking Channel per Hotel/Month
Finds which booking channel generated the highest revenue for each hotel in each month.
9. Booking Channel Share
Calculates the percentage share of total bookings for each booking channel.
10. Revenue from Millennials and Gen Z
Calculates total revenue generated by:
Millennials (born between 1980–1996)
Gen Z (born after 1996)
11. Average Stay Duration
Finds the average number of nights stayed per booking for each hotel.
12. Advance Booking Days
Calculates the average number of days in advance bookings were made for each hotel.
13. Customers with No Bookings
Lists customers who never made a booking.
14. Frequent Travelers
Finds customers who stayed in at least 3 distinct hotels in the same month. Displays customer ID, month, and hotel count.


Notes
All queries assume SQL Server syntax (e.g., DATEPART, CONVERT, square bracket identifiers).
You should ensure that foreign keys (e.g., city_id, hotel_id, customer_id) are correctly defined for optimal performance.
Always backup your data before running ALTER or UPDATE queries.
