Credit Card Spend Analysis

This analysis is focused on a dataset containing credit card transaction records across various Indian cities. Each transaction includes details such as the transaction ID, amount, transaction date, city, expense type (e.g., bills, fuel, shopping), card type (e.g., Silver, Gold, Platinum, Signature), and the gender of the cardholder. The data helps capture customer spending behavior, usage patterns, and card performance over time.

🗂️ Data Source

credit_card_transcations table:
Contains detailed information on individual credit card transactions.
Key columns: transaction_id, transaction_date, amount, city, exp_type, card_type, gender.

🎯 Objectives of the Analysis

Understand spending behavior by geography, such as top and bottom-performing cities by total spend.
Analyze monthly and seasonal trends in spending across different card types.
Identify expense types that contribute the most or least to spending in various cities.
Determine gender-based contribution, particularly how much females spend relative to the total for each expense type.
Evaluate card performance milestones, such as how quickly different card types reach a certain spend threshold.
Calculate growth trends over time, including month-over-month growth for specific card and expense combinations.
Explore transaction behavior during weekends, such as which cities see higher average spending per transaction.
Investigate transaction velocity, including how fast cities reach a certain number of transactions after their first one.

📌 Types of Insights Derived

Top-spending cities and their contribution to total credit card spend.
Peak spending months for each card type.
Cumulative spend milestones reached by card types and when they occurred.
Cities with unusually high or low spending behavior on certain card types.
Spending patterns by gender across all expense categories.
Highest growing card + expense combinations, particularly focusing on January 2014.
Weekend transaction efficiency: cities where weekend transactions yield higher value.
Speed of engagement: which city engaged most rapidly with credit cards (based on 500 transactions).

📈 Use Cases

Credit card usage analytics by financial institutions or banks.
Market segmentation based on customer location, card type, and transaction behavior.
Campaign targeting for specific expense types or demographics (e.g., high-spending weekends, female-focused offers).
Operational planning such as resource allocation based on transaction growth or density.
Fraud or anomaly detection based on unusual transaction spikes or spending ratios.
